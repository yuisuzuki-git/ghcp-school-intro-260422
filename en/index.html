About

School Overview

Kamiyama Marugoto KOSEN fosters practical skills across technology and design. Students learn by building, testing, and refining ideas with peers and mentors.

Located in Kamiyama, Tokushima, the school leverages local networks and nature-rich surroundings. Students can pursue diverse paths after graduation.

From concept to demo, classes emphasize real outcomes and collaborative workflows.
